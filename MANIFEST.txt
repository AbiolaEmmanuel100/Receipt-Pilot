Receipt Pilot mobile navigation fix

Cause
- At max-width 860px, .nav-menu was forced to display:grid.
- That later rule showed closed Sales and Business links even when .nav-drop was not open.

Fix
- Mobile .nav-menu is display:none by default.
- .nav-drop.open > .nav-menu remains display:grid.
- Service worker cache bumped to receiptly-v37 so the corrected CSS is not served from the old cache.

REPLACE
- css/styles.css
- sw.js

No HTML or JavaScript changes were required. Account button and destinations are unchanged.
