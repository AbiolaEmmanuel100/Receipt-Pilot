Frequently asked questions

Can I create a receipt without signing up?

Yes. You can create and save receipts in this browser without an account.

Can I save receipts?

Yes. Save Receipt stores the complete receipt in Receipt Records on this device. With an account, it also syncs when you are online.

Where are my receipt records stored?

Receipts are stored in this browser on this device. If you log in, they also sync to your own cloud records. Other users cannot see them. Clearing browser data can still remove the local copy.

Can I download old receipts?

Yes. Open Receipt Records, view a receipt, then download PDF or print.

Can I export my records?

Yes. Export CSV for spreadsheets, or download a JSON backup you can restore later.

Can I print receipts?

Yes. Print hides the website chrome and prints only the receipt.

Can I add my business logo?

Yes. Upload an image in Business Settings and save.

Can I use different currencies?

Choose NGN, USD, GBP, EUR, GHS or KES. The currency you select appears on receipts and reports.
